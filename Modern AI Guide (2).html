FUNDAMENTALS

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by computer systems. It involves learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding.

CORE TECH

What is ML?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subfield of AI. It gives computers the ability to learn and improve from data experiences automatically, without being explicitly programmed to perform the task.

USE CASES

Where is AI used?

AI is everywhere: virtual assistants (Siri, Alexa), personalized recommendations (Netflix, Spotify), autonomous driving, medical diagnosis, and fraud detection in banking.
